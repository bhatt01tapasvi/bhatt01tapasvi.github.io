Motorcycles

Three bikes, three cities.

Started with a Hero Glamour 125cc back in Jaipur, nothing fast but where I picked up basic wrenching: carburetor, plugs, engine tone. Moved up to an Ignitor for the Jaipur-to-Pilani runs, until it got stolen. Now in Singapore I ride a Honda CB190X, the max-spec setup the local 200cc license cap allows. Upright ergonomics, stable suspension, and clean throttle response, mostly ridden on empty late-night roads.
